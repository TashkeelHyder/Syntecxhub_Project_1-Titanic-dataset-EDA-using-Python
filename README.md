# Syntecxhub_Project_1-Titanic-dataset-EDA-using-Python
Analyzed the Titanic Dataset using Python
1.	Gender-Based Survival: There was a massive disparity in survival between genders. While there were nearly double the number of males (577) compared to females (314), women survived at a much higher rate (~74%) compared to men (~18.8%), confirming the "women and children first" protocol.

2.	Socio-Economic Impact (Class): Passenger Class played a critical role in survival. 1st Class passengers had the highest survival rate, whereas 3rd Class passengers, who made up the majority of the ship (491 people), faced the highest number of casualties.

3.	Age as a Factor: Children (ages 0–10) were the only age group where the number of survivors exceeded the number of deaths. Conversely, young adults in the 20–30 age bucket represented the largest group on the ship but also suffered the highest total number of deaths.

4.	Solo vs. Family Travelers: The majority of passengers were solo travelers (over 600 people with 0 siblings/spouses). Data indicates that passengers traveling with small families (1–3 members) generally had better survival prospects than those traveling completely alone or in very large groups

5.	Port of Embarkation: Over 70% of passengers boarded at Southampton (S). While most casualties also came from this group due to its size, passengers boarding at Cherbourg (C) showed a slightly higher survival trend, likely because a larger portion of them were in 1st Class.
